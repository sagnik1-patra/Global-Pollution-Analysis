FOOD DELIVERY TIME PREDICTION AND DELAY CLASSIFICATION REPORT
======================================================================

PROJECT OBJECTIVE
- Predict food delivery time using delivery-related features.
- Classify deliveries as Fast or Delayed.
- Generate operational insights for routes and staffing.

DATASET INFORMATION
Dataset Shape: (200, 23)
Total Records: 200
Delivery Delay Threshold: 72.775

FEATURE ENGINEERING
- Haversine distance calculated from customer and restaurant coordinates.
- Rush Hour and Non-Rush Hour feature created from Order_Time.
- Delivery_Status created using median Delivery_Time.

OPERATIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS
- Increase delivery staff during lunch and dinner rush hours.
- Assign nearest available delivery partners to high-priority orders.
- Use traffic-aware route planning during high traffic periods.
- Prefer faster vehicle types for short-distance and urgent deliveries.
- Avoid assigning long-distance deliveries to inexperienced delivery partners during rush hours.
- Monitor locations with repeated delays and increase local delivery coverage.
- Use predicted delivery time to show realistic ETA to customers.
- Create separate staffing plans for rush hour and non-rush hour demand.
